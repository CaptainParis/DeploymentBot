# Athena

Athena simplifies the process of creating, managing, and executing deployments in the 101st, handling everything from automated group formation to voice channel management. Designed to maximize efficiency and adaptability, Athena can sort and manage 500+ people at once for battalion-sized operations.

Athena was initially designed to streamline deployments within the 101st, but its true legacy is in the destruction of that very unit. Full credit goes to Deathtrooper and NorthWatchman for delivering the final blow ;).

With that chapter now closed, I’m making the project public. While Athena served its purpose, I’m now focused on developing something far superior that will render this bot obsolete. However, Athena is available as-is, and contributions are welcome to keep it alive.

Some Features:
    Automated Deployment Management: Manage group formation, scheduling, and deployment announcements with ease.
    Hot Drop Functionality: Last-minute additions can be seamlessly integrated into ongoing deployments.
    Voice Channel Management: Automatically create and delete voice channels based on deployment status.
    Failsafes and Error Handling: Built for smooth operation even under high-traffic situations.
